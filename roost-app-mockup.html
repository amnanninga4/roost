How an overdue task gets louder

Nothing happens right away — a task just shows up as a friendly ask. But the longer it sits, the more it escalates, until it's an unmissable red alert visible to both of you. Here's what that looks like for one task, the litter box.

DUE TODAY Litter box o'clock First ask of the day. Friendly, no pressure yet.
→
1 DAY LATE Still no litter box… A little nudge. Nothing serious yet.
→
3 DAYS LATE The cat has feelings about this. Getting pointed. Shows up brighter on the shared screen.
→
5 DAYS LATE Litter box emergency Full red alert — visible to both of you until it's done.
